The Context

Why farmers need an earlier signal

Crop disease is preventable when caught early. Today, most small farms have no way to see it coming.

What the project does

Crop diseases are one of the leading causes of yield loss worldwide, and most farmers lack the tools to catch them in the early stages.

AgriSense monitors temperature, humidity, and soil moisture in real time through low-cost IoT sensors, then feeds that data to an AI model that predicts disease risk before visible symptoms appear.

The problem, in four parts

  • Late detection. Symptoms are often noticed only after damage is irreversible.
  • Inaccurate inspection. Manual, visual checks are slow and subjective.
  • Excess pesticide. Without precise data, farmers over-spray, harming soil and raising costs.
  • No continuous monitoring. Small-scale farms rarely have access to real-time field data.

The Process

From soil to smartphone

Four stages, running continuously, turn raw field conditions into an actionable warning.

01

Field monitoring

Sensors capture soil moisture, temperature, and humidity around the clock.

02

Data transmission

An ESP32 microcontroller processes readings and pushes them to the cloud over Wi-Fi.

03

AI analysis

A trained machine-learning model scans incoming data for early disease patterns.

04

Smart alert

The farmer receives a risk score and a clear, actionable recommendation.
